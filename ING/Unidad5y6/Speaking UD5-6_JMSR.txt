E: Jose, here’s your work plan for next week. Your days off will be Monday and Wednesday and on Friday you’ll be working off-site. On Saturday morning, I want you to drop something at London’s office.

J: Ok, where is the London’s office? I can’t remember.

E: It’s right outside of the train station, in the alley. They need to get installed a new program for work management.

J: Sure boss. Which software should I use?

E: The IT department said they have it ready for you on a hard drive. You only have to insert the hard drive, transfer it to their computers and follow the instructions on the screen.

J: OK, no problem. What else needs to be done?

E: On Tuesday I need you to do some data recovering for the Human Resources department. 

J: How do I get the access to their backup system?

E: I will send an email to you with the credentials necessary. Is everything clear?

J: I think so, thank you Ester.

E: Thank you, keep me updated.
